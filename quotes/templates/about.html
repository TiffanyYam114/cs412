About This App

This application displays quotes and images of Oscar Isaac.

Oscar Isaac is a Guatemalan actor. He is known for breaking stereotypes about Latino actors in Hollywood. He has played a variety of nationalities, including Egyption, Indonesian, and English. He has amazing range, and I think he's a great actor. My favorite film of his is Moon Knight.
